Job Hunt

I love seeing those Sankey diagrams online that summarise a long job search, and it turns out they're very easy to make with mermaid.js, so I threw one together, and will update as my search for full-time work out of university continues.

I enjoy the concept that the worse the job search goes, the prettier a graph I'll get to look at, as consolation! 

---
config:
  sankey:
    nodeAlignment: justify
---
sankey
