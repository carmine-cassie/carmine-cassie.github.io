About Me!

Hello! Hi! I'm Cassie, and this is my website!

I find most personal websites have an "About Me" section, which, I struggled to write without being, like, self-aggrandising, so I populate this section with things I'm interested in. Perhaps an unsurprising feat of selection bias, hey, that I think my interests are the most interesting thing about me, and hopefully they reflect enough on me to serve an "About Me" function here.

My interests include:

  • Computer Science: I did a whole dang degree on this! It's very fun to solve problems and learn things and make things. It's tricky to make sense of, though, with the world at the moment, as I move into the professional sector I'm seeing a less hobbyist, more profit-driven lens, which brings out its darker side, and quells some of the reasons I fell in love with it.
  • VFX, CGI, those art forms that happen via transparent matrix multiplications inside of the 'puter! I love the way my exposure to this stuff has made me think about optics and how light works - light is very gorgeous stuff. Everybody should be thinking about the raytracing equation.
  • Linguistics is good stuff, too. There's a pattern to things I'm interested in, hey: computers, light, language. I like paying attention to some of the things we touch thousands of times a day, and learning about the beautiful and underlooked systems that drive them. It's just so satisfying to know how vowels work, and linguistics fun facts are some of the best.
  • The color red.
  • Reading is quite delightful, I've been finding recently, thanks to my little 15-year-old ereader. You can check out what I've been reading here!
  • Been getting into musical theatre recently, too. My top three would probably be Ghost Quartet, Hadestown, and Cabaret. As a result, I sing a lot, which has been really lovely from a body positivity standpoint - I like my voice!
  • Typesetting and bookbinding is also fun, sometimes I play with Typst or a bone folder!

If you want to contact me, you can reach me at cass at (this domain)
