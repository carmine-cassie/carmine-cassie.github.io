lipu loje pi kon nimi (WIP)

I have a lot of thoughts and feelings about toki pona, and, because it has so few words, I have a lot of thoughts and feelings about almost all of them.

I thought it'd be fun to collect these thoughts together, as they cross my mind! (Ordered by most-recent first.) I'll make the page public once I've got, like, five here?

[2026-08-21]

tonsi is when you see another visibly queer person on the bus and feel a little bit less alone. Sometimes, people dislike this word, perhaps because, in its breadth, it collapses many experiences into one word. Isn't that one of the most beautiful parts of this language, though? The way it presents these vast, open definitions that hold too much significance to be expressed any other way?

You can always capture specificity with description, but no narrower word can express the instant connection and sense of safety in familiarity and love I have for my community the way that my usage of tonsi does.

I think that tonsi sings of the feeling Torrey Peters spends a novella circling in Infect Your Friends and Loved Ones in tones much purer than "T4T".
