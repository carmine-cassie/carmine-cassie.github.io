Blog

...or Journal?

Each month, I do a little write-up of all the little projects I completed that month, as well as books I read, things like that. I originally intended it as blog, in that, its purpose was to be read by others, that my friends could keep abreast of my movements.

As I've written them, though, month-over-month, I find that most of their value is in how they permit me to keep track of time, get my thoughts in order, and become motivated to actually finish projects. They've a more introspective utility than I initially anticipated, which perhaps makes them a bit more journally? Who's to say!

Every now and then I write something that isn't a monthly recap, though, and those are definitely more bloggy :>
