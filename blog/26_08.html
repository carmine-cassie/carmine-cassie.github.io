What I Read this Month!

Twig Arc 18: Dog Eat Dog

The end of the last chapter of this arc made me cry, and I don't think I've cried from a book since I was a kid and got freaked out by how well Bod sold his "father's" death to that policeman in The Graveyard Book! (Though the exam pressure did presumably lower my tears threshold.)

I mean this one keeps up the trend, hey, arc-over-arc, Wildbow manages to tug further and further on my heartstrings with each depiction of just how bad shape the Lambs are in. Like, we get the glimpses of the growths and knots in Mary's back for the first time, and of Helen's brain-body connection breaking down (loved that chapter). And, of course, Sy. Gosh, the part early on in the arc where Sy sees Helen eviscerate the three blind mice spooked me, super haunting, though really the only thing on my mind as I write this is the arc's ending.

Just, the little section where the Lambs return, and Sy is asking whether he's killed people and Jessie responds "No, Sy." each time really got me. Like, the pairing of his absolute trust in her with her patience for him, so soon after he was musing about how Jessie feels like the only "real" relationship he's had because they each needed the other. AND there's the "Lillian made a small sound" as the questions continue, because she hasn't seen him in so long, she hasn't seen how bad it's gotten. I don't know, dude, it really hit me. Something about the depiction of trust and patience and also of ignorance, framed against what the characters are all going through together. I am going to miss these characters so much after I finish the next two arcs.

Tanakh: Torah/Genesis

It's so interesting be, like, vaguely culturally aware of so many of the stories here, but only now be actually reading them. Like it's interesting to see how things were omitted or changed in the tellings that have reached me via the oral Christian background radiation.

Like, the Noah's ark section? The wacky pacing and contradictory details from the way it was combined from multiple sources are really interesting things that I never knew about.
