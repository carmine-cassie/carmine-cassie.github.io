Bolognese, Oh Yeah

Dice veggies whenever you got a chance. I do like like 1 onion per 4 people, 2 carrots per 4 people, 1 courgette/zucchini per 4 people. Do em in that order, because the onions go in first, and the carrots take longer to soften than the courgette.

Once the onion is diced, chuck it in a big big pot with oil (olive oil has an Italian vibe), fry it til it's clear. If it sounds angry turn it down. Move it around a bit with a wooden spoon while it cooks. (I love wooden spoon.) Also chuck in like a teaspon of jarlic (jar garlic) if ur feelin' it.

Once the onion is clear put in some mince! I use 500g per 4 people, any mince works, I get cheap beef. Break it up with the wooden spoon so that it becomes ko more than leko.

Once it's all brown, pour in like half a bottle of passata and one can of crushed (or diced) tomato per four people. You can use water to get extra out of this. Add in the diced carrots and courgette, and maybe a bunch of "Italian herbs and spices".

Mix, cover with a clear lid, turn the heat down once it starts simmering, and leave it for ages. If the veggies aren't diced yet it's okay to add them a little later, but the carrot needs time to soften.

I put a little water in the pasta pot while the rest is in the kettle so the pot can warm up while the water does. Once it's all in and boiling, add 1 bag of pasta per 4 people and set a phone timer for like 10 minutes. Give it a little mix with a pasta spoon at some point so it doesn't stick. When ther timer goes off, strain it (you can use colander or lid for a skill check).
