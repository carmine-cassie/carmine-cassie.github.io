Reading List

Frequently, I hear about a book and go "oooh I should read that". But reading takes time, and I'm scared I'll forget about the books I want to read! To that end, I maintain a little list of books I want to read on this page.

If you've any book recommendations, reach out to me!

Unfinished series. I'm waiting for more instalments to release. (At the top of the list so I can check for things I'm waiting on before picking up something new.)

  • This Used to Be About Dungeons, by Alexander Wales, is releasing a few more volumes, one a year or so.

Authors I like! I can work through their material or keep an eye out for new releases. (Like the unfinished series!)

  • Emily Zhou, who wrote Girlfriends, a short story collection I liked, is apparently working on an untitled novel!
  • Wildbow has written several huge serials I've loved. If I want to dig into something I can always check out Ward, Pale, Claw, or the unfinished Seek.
  • qntm I've enjoyed some of the work of. I can try Ra or Fine Structure.
  • I've also enjoyed some ofJonathan Sims' work. I can check out Family Business or The Burn Line.

Things I'm aware of, and want to try (or try again).

  • Dune, by Frank Herbert - I love the movies but couldn't get through the book as a child.
  • Discworld, by Terry Pratchett.
  • A Short History of Nearly Everything, by Bill Bryson. (Second edition?!) I read the shorter, illustrated version as a kid.
  • The Communist Manifesto, by Marx and Engels. Everyone is always talking about Marx and this seems short and sweet, so it might make it to my eyes at some point.
  • Maybe I should re-read The Lord of The Rings??? (By Tolkien.) When I read it as a kid, I stopped right before the scouring of the shire!

Recommended by friends.

  • To Sleep in a Sea of Stars, by Christopher Paolini.
  • The Secret History, by Donna Tartt.
  • The Farseer Trilogy, by Robin Hobb.
  • Watership Down, by Richard Adams.

Mentioned online.

  • Little Blue Encyclopedia, by Hazel Jane Plante.
  • Focault's Pendulum, by Umberto Eco.
  • The Left Hand of Darkness, by Ursula K. Le Guin.
  • Warhound: Volume One, by Kallidora Rho.
